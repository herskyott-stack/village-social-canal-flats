Coffee & kitchen · Canal Flats, BC

The little café at the head of Columbia Lake.

Village Social is Canal Flats’ family-run coffee-and-kitchen on Columbia Ave — espresso and baked goods in the morning, scratch-made breakfast and lunch till early afternoon, and a patio made for slow Kootenay days.

4939 Columbia Ave, Canal Flats, BC · Wed–Sun 9:30–2:30 · The daily word lives on Instagram

Coffee, Properly

Espresso drinks and brewed coffee to start the day — lakeside mornings deserve better than gas-station coffee.

Scratch Kitchen

Breakfast sandwiches, garden salads, a chicken pesto melt worth the drive — and specials like the Benny & the Jets benedict when the kitchen is feeling playful.

Baked In-House

Muffins, hand pies (the cherry ones go fast) and whatever else came out of the oven that morning.

Family-Run

A small family operation with deep local roots — the kind of place where your order is remembered by your second visit.

Hours

Wednesday – Sunday: 9:30 AM – 2:30 PM
Monday & Tuesday: closed
